About Real Product Origin

Real Product Origin is the company behind Product Origin Checker. We build tools that bring transparency to consumer purchasing decisions — starting with the country-of-origin question, which Amazon makes deliberately hard to answer.

We're an early-stage project. If you're a journalist covering supply chains, an industry insider with knowledge we should be hearing, or a developer who wants to help — we'd love to hear from you.
