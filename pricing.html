A product check is the first time you score a distinct product. A brand check is the first time you look up a distinct company. They are separate allowances — checking a product never uses a brand check. Re-opening either one you have already checked is free, always, including after your allowance runs out. Full definitions in the Terms.

The public record on a brand — who registered the name, in which country, and where the website is registered — is free to everyone, on every tier, with no limit. A brand check is only used for our deeper assessment of who ultimately owns the company.

Common questions.

Do I need to create an account to use the free tier?

No. Install the extension and your first 20 checks work anonymously. You only sign in when you want to subscribe or use the mobile app.

What counts as a "check"?

One product page on a supported retailer (see the full list) that you view with the extension enabled. Cached hits — repeat visits to the same product within 90 days — do not count against your monthly quota.

Which online retailers do you support?

Thirty-three retailers across 32 domains today — covering general retail (Amazon, Target, Walmart), home improvement, home & furniture, electronics, warehouse membership, fashion, beauty, and auto parts. See the full list with categories, or tell us which one to add next.

Do I need to pay to see your sources?

No. Every tier — including Free — shows the sources behind every claim, and every tier can contest a score. Sourcing is expensive on our side, but a score you can't check isn't worth much, so the evidence is never the thing we charge for. What you pay for is how many products you can check.

What's included in the mobile app?

Barcode scanning on both iOS and Android — point your camera at a product in a physical store, get the same country-of-origin score you'd see on a supported online retailer. The apps are free on every tier; what you can check follows your plan, exactly as it does in the extension.

How do I upgrade or downgrade?

Any time from the Stripe Customer Portal, linked from the extension popup. Upgrades are prorated instantly; downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

How do I cancel?

Through Stripe's Customer Portal, linked directly from the extension popup and mobile app. Cancel any time; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — full refund within 14 days of your first charge, no questions asked. After that, cancel any time; we don't refund partial periods.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Apple Pay and Google Pay also work in the Stripe checkout flow.

Family or team pricing?

Not yet. If enough people ask, we'll build it — get in touch and let us know your use case.
