About us

Why we built this.

Real Product Origin was started by people who have watched, up close, what happens when unchecked state power meets unchecked commerce.

Who we are

Activists, researchers, engineers, survivors.

Between us we count family members, close friends, and colleagues who have been wrongfully detained, tortured, or killed by the Chinese Communist regime for their beliefs. Some of us have been imprisoned ourselves. Some have been silenced. All of us have spent years — in some cases decades — documenting what happens behind the walls of that country's prisons and camps, and building networks of survivors and witnesses to keep those records intact.

We do not, on this page, name the individuals or the community we come from. Doing so would put family members still inside the country at risk. What we can say is that our lived experience of state violence, and our long practice of documenting it, is what led us here.

What we saw

Ordinary channels ran out.

For most of our lives we tried to raise the alarm through the ordinary channels. Testimony before parliaments. Reports for human-rights bodies. Documentaries. Diplomatic lobbying. Some of it landed. Most of it didn't. The regime kept expanding, kept persecuting, and — critically — kept exporting.

At some point the pattern became impossible to ignore. Every dollar a Western shopper spends on a product manufactured under a system of forced labour, censorship, or state coercion is a dollar that funds the machinery we have spent our lives fighting. The shopper isn't complicit — they can't be, they don't know. The product page tells them nothing. The label is designed to hide, not reveal. The retailer profits from the ambiguity. And the money flows, quiet and constant, into the coffers of a state that treats its own people as raw material.

Meanwhile the international response has been thin. Trade agreements ignore the coercive foundations of what they trade. Human-rights language is stripped out at the drafting stage. Governments that could act, don't. Governments that would act, can't — because their economies are too entangled. The political will to intervene at any scale sufficient to matter has been absent for a generation, and shows no sign of arriving.

Why transparency

Information is the one lever that scales.

We considered many strategies. Lawsuits. Boycotts. Treaties. Corporate pressure campaigns. Some of them we still work on. But the one thing that scales — the one thing that doesn't require the permission of any government or the goodwill of any retailer — is information itself.

Every purchase is a vote. Every dollar signals what the shopper is willing to support. The market, at scale, is the largest continuous referendum in human history. But a vote cast in ignorance is not a vote at all. It is manipulation, dressed up as choice.

Real Product Origin exists to give shoppers back the vote. Where is this product actually made. Who actually manufactured it. Where does the money actually go. Not what the label wants you to think. What is really true.

What we're building

A transparency layer for the marketplace you already shop.

Available as a browser extension today, with a mobile app on the way. Sourced from public records, cross-checked against structured databases, and — where evidence is thin — presented with confidence bands that admit what we don't know. No greenwashing. No manufactured trust. Just the fact of a product's provenance, put in front of you at the moment you decide whether to click Buy.

This is not the whole solution. It is one practical step in a much longer effort. If enough people, at scale, start knowing what they're buying, the market will begin to sort itself.

That is what we are here to do.
