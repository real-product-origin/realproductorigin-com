Install

Real Product Origin runs as a free browser extension on desktop and as a mobile app on your phone. Use either or both — same account, same scores.

Browser extension

How to install (about 30 seconds)
  1. Click Add to Chrome (or Edge / Brave). Approve the permissions prompt — the extension only reads pages on supported retailer domains.
  2. Pin the extension so its badge always shows in your toolbar: click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right and pin Real Product Origin.
  3. Open any product page on one of the 25 supported retailers. A score badge appears in the top-right within a couple of seconds.

Understand the score

The most common question isn't "how do I install" — it's "why did you say that?" Here's what every number and flag on the score card means. Every score is produced by Claude AI reading the product page in full and cross-checking against trademark filings, customs records, seller storefronts, and brand websites — with each claim cited so you can check the work.

The four indicators

Every product is scored across four questions. They can each point at a different country, and often do.

1 Made in
Where the physical product was manufactured. Comes from labels, brand disclosures, customs records, trademark filings, and brand-family knowledge. Not the same as where it's assembled or shipped from.
2 Ships from
Where the actual inventory sits before you buy it. On Amazon this is often a US fulfillment center regardless of manufacture. Useful for supply-chain and shipping-emissions context, not for who profits.
3 Retailer
The company that takes your payment. Either one of our 25 supported retailers, or a third-party marketplace seller — in which case, where that seller is based.
4 Money goes to
The beneficial owner — the parent company or brand family that ultimately receives the profit. Many household brand names roll up to a small number of multinational holding groups. We surface the ultimate parent, not just the label on the box.

What the confidence percentage means

Each indicator gets a percentage. Higher means we found more corroborating evidence from independent sources; lower means we found some evidence but not enough to be sure. A confidence below 40% is shown but flagged as tentative — treat it as a lead, not a conclusion.

What "Not specified" means

Sometimes a row shows Not specified instead of a country. This means none of our sources — product page, seller history, brand records, trademark filings, customs data, category priors — surfaced a confident answer for that indicator. It's an honest gap, not a guess. We'd rather show nothing than invent an answer.

What "Ultimately: [company]" means

The retailer or brand name you see on a listing is often part of a larger corporate family. Ultimately: [company] tells you the top-of-chain parent. This matters most for the Money goes to indicator — who profits from a purchase isn't always the name on the label.

Troubleshooting

The specific problems people run into most often. Click any question to expand.

The extension badge doesn't appear on a product page

Try in this order:

  1. Reload the page. Amazon in particular does a lot of client-side navigation, and the extension sometimes needs a fresh page load to attach.
  2. Check the URL. The extension only runs on individual product pages (URLs containing /dp/, /gp/product/, /p/, or /ip/). Category pages, search results, and cart pages are skipped intentionally.
  3. Check the category. We skip a set of categories where country-of-origin scoring doesn't apply — books, digital content, Prime Video, Kindle, apps, gift cards. That's deliberate.
  4. Confirm the extension is enabled. Right-click the toolbar icon → Manage extension → make sure the switch is on.
  5. Still nothing? Send us the URL and we'll investigate.
Every product I check shows "Unknown"

This usually means the extension can't reach our backend. Possible causes:

  • Corporate or school network blocking api.realproductorigin.com — check with your IT team.
  • Ad-blocker or privacy extension over-blocking — try disabling it on the retailer page temporarily.
  • Backend outage on our side (rare) — ask us.

If the same product later scores correctly for other users, it's almost certainly a network-side block.

I can't sign in — it just loops back to the sign-in page

The magic link should sign you in automatically when you click it. If it doesn't:

  1. Check which browser you clicked the link in. The link only signs you in in the browser where you requested it. If it opened in a different default browser, use the "paste token" option on the sign-in page to complete manually.
  2. Reopen the extension popup. After signing in on the marketing site, the extension picks up the session on the next popup open — close and reopen it.
  3. Try incognito. If a strict tracking-protection setting is blocking the handoff, incognito bypasses it long enough to identify the culprit.
I hit "quota exceeded" on the Free tier

Free tier includes 20 scans per rolling 30-day window. The counter resets on the anniversary of each scan, not on the first of the month. Options:

  • Wait for older scans to age out — each individual scan re-frees itself 30 days after it was made.
  • Upgrade to Basic ($3/mo) for 200 scans, Pro ($9/mo) for unlimited scans plus contests and priority support, or Premium for team features. See pricing.
  • Redeem an RPO-XXXX-XXXX access code if you have one. Codes stack on top of any paid plan.
Sharing from the Amazon app on mobile shows an error

You want the latest app version installed — earlier builds only supported full Amazon URLs, but the Amazon app usually shares a shortened a.co link. Recent builds resolve any shortener (a.co, amzn.to, t.co, bit.ly, and more) automatically.

If you're on the latest version and still see an error, please send us the exact URL that failed. We add shorteners as we spot them.

A score in the extension doesn't match a score in the mobile app

This can happen when the mobile flow provides less information than the extension does. The extension scrapes the entire product page — title, seller, breadcrumbs, brand blurb, technical details. A mobile share (or barcode / photo lookup) starts from just an identifier and asks the backend to figure out the rest.

When mobile has fewer inputs, the score is honest about being less certain — lower confidence, more "Not specified" rows, or a straight "Unknown product" for obscure items. Cross-check by opening the same product on desktop.

Mobile-only scores are not cached — every mobile lookup runs fresh — precisely so a less-complete guess never poisons the shared cache used by extension users.

The mobile app camera won't open for barcode or photo

Grant camera permission the first time you tap Scan or Snap. If it was denied accidentally:

  • iOS — Settings → Product Origin Checker → Camera → toggle on.
  • Android — long-press the app icon → App info → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
I purchased a plan but it isn't reflected

Stripe usually posts the upgrade within a few seconds. If after a couple of minutes your account still shows the old tier:

  1. Refresh your account page.
  2. Sign out and back in on the extension popup — the extension caches your plan for the current popup session.
  3. Still stuck? Email hello@realproductorigin.com with the email you used at checkout and we'll trace it manually.
