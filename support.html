Install

Real Product Origin lives in two places — a free browser extension that runs on product pages as you shop, and a mobile app that scores anything you share, scan, or search. You can use either or both.

Browser extension

Works on Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Brave today. Firefox is submitted and pending review. Same extension code, same score, same account.

Install for Chrome → Install for Edge → Install for Brave → Firefox — pending review
  1. Click the install button for your browser above. You'll land on the Chrome Web Store listing (Edge and Brave both install from there too).
  2. Click Add to Chrome (or "Add to Brave" / "Get" on Edge). Approve the permissions prompt — the extension only reads pages on supported retailer domains.
  3. Pin the extension so its badge always shows in the toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the top right, find Real Product Origin, click the pin.
  4. Open any product page on any of our 12 supported retailers. The score appears automatically in the top-right corner within a couple of seconds.

Score a product

On desktop the extension does the work for you — just browse. On mobile you have five ways to score, depending on where you are and what you're holding.

On the extension — it's automatic

The extension runs quietly on every Amazon, Target, Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, Wayfair, Best Buy, Costco, Nordstrom, Macy's, Sephora, and RockAuto product page. When it recognizes a product, it displays a badge in the top-right of the page with:

  • An overall trust label — colored ring around the score summarizing how much of the value chain avoids concerning jurisdictions.
  • Four rows — Made in / Ships from / Retailer / Money goes to, each with a country flag and a confidence percentage.
  • Ultimate parent — if the seller is owned by a larger company, who that is (e.g. many brands roll up to a handful of holding companies).

Click the badge to expand it. Click "How was this scored?" inside to see every source we used.

Understand the score

The most common support question isn't "how do I install" — it's "why did you say that?" Here's what every number and flag on the score card means.

The four indicators

Every product is scored across four questions. They can each point at a different country, and often do.

1 Made in
Where the physical product was manufactured. Comes from labels, brand disclosures, customs records, trademark filings, and brand-family knowledge. Not the same as where it's assembled or shipped from.
2 Ships from
Where the actual inventory sits before you buy it. On Amazon this is often a US fulfillment center regardless of manufacture. Useful for supply-chain and shipping-emissions context, not for who profits.
3 Retailer
The company that takes your payment. Any of the 12 retailers we support (Amazon, Target, Walmart, Home Depot, Lowe's, Wayfair, Best Buy, Costco, Nordstrom, Macy's, Sephora, RockAuto) or a third-party marketplace seller. If a third-party marketplace seller — where they're based.
4 Money goes to
The beneficial owner — the parent company or brand family that ultimately receives the profit. Many household brand names roll up to a small number of multinational holding groups. We surface the ultimate parent, not just the label on the box.

What confidence means

Each indicator gets a percentage. Higher means we found more corroborating evidence from independent sources; lower means we found some evidence but not enough to be sure. A confidence below 40% is shown but flagged as tentative — treat it as a lead, not a conclusion.

What "Not specified" means

Sometimes a row shows "Not specified" instead of a country. This means none of our sources — product page, seller history, brand records, trademark filings, customs data, category priors — surfaced a confident answer for that indicator. It's an honest gap, not a guess. We'd rather show nothing than invent an answer.

What "Ultimately: [company]" means

The retailer or brand name you see on a listing is often part of a larger corporate family. "Ultimately: [company]" tells you the top-of-chain parent. This matters for the Money goes to indicator, because who profits from a purchase isn't always the name on the label.

Report an issue

Wrong scores, missing features, weird bugs, privacy concerns — every one of these matters and every one has a direct path in. Pick the one that fits and skip the guessing game.

A score is wrong

Most common. If a product's country or confidence looks off, this is the fastest way to get it corrected.

  1. Click the Contest button in the extension popup (available on Pro; see pricing).
  2. Tell us which indicator is wrong (Made in, Ships from, Retailer, or Money goes to) and what you believe the right answer is.
  3. If you have a source, paste it in — a product-page screenshot, a manufacturer's website, an official disclosure. We use these directly.
  4. We review contested scores manually and update the brand-family record when we agree. Corrections propagate to every future scan of that product or brand.

On Free tier or want to send us a longer note? Email hello@realproductorigin.com with the product URL and the correction.

Something is broken

Extension won't load, badge stuck spinning, mobile app crashes, a button doesn't do anything — those are bugs, and we squash them fast.

  1. Open the contact form.
  2. Pick "Bug report" as the topic.
  3. Include: the URL where it happened, your browser or OS, and — if you can — a screenshot or a description of what you were trying to do.

  • Name the retailer or brand and, if it's a retailer, share the country you shop from. Country availability matters — some sites have per-country stock, pricing, and origin data.

Troubleshooting

The specific problems people run into most often. Click any question to expand.

The extension badge doesn't appear on a product page

Try in this order:

  1. Reload the page. Amazon in particular does a lot of client-side navigation, and the extension sometimes needs a fresh page load to attach.
  2. Check the URL. The extension only runs on individual product pages (URLs containing /dp/, /gp/product/, /p/, or /ip/). Category pages, search results, and cart pages are skipped intentionally.
  3. Check the category. We skip a set of categories where country-of-origin scoring doesn't apply — books, digital content, Prime Video, Kindle, apps, gift cards. That's deliberate, not a bug.
  4. Confirm the extension is enabled. Right-click the toolbar icon → Manage extension → make sure the switch is on.
  5. Still nothing? Send us the URL and we'll investigate.
Every product I check shows "Unknown"

This usually means the extension can't reach our backend. Possible causes:

  • Corporate or school network blocking api.realproductorigin.com — check with your IT team.
  • Ad-blocker or privacy extension over-blocking — try disabling it on the retailer page temporarily.
  • Backend outage on our side (rare) — check our status or ask us.

If the same product later scores correctly for other users, it's almost certainly a network-side block.

I can't sign in — it just loops back to the sign-in page

The magic link should sign you in automatically when you click it in your email. If it doesn't:

  1. Check which browser you clicked the link in — the link only signs you in in the browser where you requested it. If it opened in a different default browser, use the "paste token" option on the sign-in page to complete manually.
  2. Check the extension is unlocked — after signing in on the marketing site, the extension picks up the session on next popup open. Close and reopen the popup.
  3. Try incognito — if a strict tracking-protection setting is blocking the handoff, incognito bypasses it long enough to identify the culprit.
I hit "quota exceeded" on the Free tier

Free tier includes 20 scans per rolling 30-day window. The counter resets on the anniversary of each scan, not on the first of the month. Options:

  • Wait for older scans to age out (each individual scan re-frees itself 30 days after it was made).
  • Upgrade to Basic ($3/mo) for 200 scans, Pro ($9/mo) for unlimited scans plus contests and priority support, or Premium for team features. See pricing.
  • Redeem an RPO-XXXX-XXXX access code if you have one. Codes stack on top of any paid plan.
Sharing from the Amazon app on mobile shows an error

You want the latest app version installed — earlier builds only supported full Amazon URLs, but the Amazon app usually shares a shortened a.co link. Recent builds resolve any shortener (a.co, amzn.to, t.co, bit.ly, and more) automatically.

If you're on the latest version and still see an error, please send us the exact URL that failed and the error text. We add shorteners as we spot them.

A score in the extension doesn't match a score in the mobile app for the same product

This is possible if the mobile flow provided less information than the extension did. The extension scrapes the entire product page — title, seller, breadcrumbs, brand blurb, technical details. A mobile share (or barcode / photo lookup) starts from just an identifier and asks our backend to figure out the rest.

When mobile has fewer inputs, the score is honest about being less certain — you'll see lower confidence numbers, more "Not specified" rows, or a straight "Unknown product" for obscure items we don't recognize. If a mobile score comes back suspiciously wrong, the extension will typically get closer. Cross-check by opening the same product on desktop.

We don't cache mobile-only scores — every mobile lookup runs fresh — precisely so a less-complete guess never poisons the shared cache used by extension users.

The mobile app camera won't open for barcode or photo

You'll need to grant camera permission the first time you tap Scan or Snap. If you accidentally denied it:

  • iOS — Settings → Product Origin Checker → Camera → toggle on.
  • Android — long-press the app icon → App info → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
I purchased a plan but it isn't reflected

Stripe usually posts the upgrade to your account within a few seconds. If after a couple of minutes your account still shows Free or the old tier:

  1. Refresh your account page.
  2. Sign out and back in on the extension popup — the extension caches your plan for the current popup session.
  3. If it still hasn't updated, email hello@realproductorigin.com with the email you used at checkout and we'll trace it manually.
