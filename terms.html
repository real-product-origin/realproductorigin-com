2. The Service

Product Origin Checker is a tool that estimates transparency indicators for products listed on large online retailers — currently Amazon, Target, Walmart, and 27 other major retailers, with more added over time. The indicators cover where the product was manufactured, where the unit ships from, who the retailer of record is, and where the money ultimately goes. The Service is operated by Real Product Origin.

3. Scores are estimates, not facts

This is the most important thing to understand. Our country-of-origin scores are probabilistic estimates derived from publicly available product information and AI inference. They are accompanied by an explicit confidence band that communicates how certain we are.

  • A 95% / 90% confidence score is our best, well-supported estimate. It is not a factual assertion that the product is from that country.
  • A 50% / 20% confidence score communicates that we genuinely cannot determine the origin with the evidence available.
  • Manually-verified scores (marked "✓ Verified") have been reviewed by a human but remain subject to error.

You agree to interpret scores in that spirit. You agree not to represent any score as a definitive factual statement about a product, a seller, or a company.
